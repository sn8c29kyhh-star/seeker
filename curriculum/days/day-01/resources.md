# 📚 Day 1 Resources & References

Curated reading material, cheatsheets, and interactive tools for Day 1 topics.

---

## 📖 Mandatory Reading

1. **[The Missing Semester of Your CS Education - The Shell](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/2020/course-shell/)** (MIT CSAIL)
   - Exceptional lecture notes and videos on shell basics, redirection, and pipeline navigation.
2. **[Linux Journey - Command Line & Permissions](https://linuxjourney.com/)**
   - Interactive, bite-sized lessons explaining permissions, users, and the Linux file system hierarchy.

---

## 🧰 Cheatsheets & Reference

- **[Explainshell.com](https://explainshell.com/)**: Paste any complex bash command, and it breaks down what each flag and argument does.
- **[Devhints Bash Cheatsheet](https://devhints.io/bash)**: Concise reference for bash conditionals, loops, and parameters.
- **[Bash Hackers Wiki](https://wiki.bash-hackers.org/)**: In-depth explanations of bash syntax nuances.
