## A simple daily rhythm

1. **Learn.** Read the lesson and explain the main ideas in your own words.
2. **Practise.** Work through the exercise and save evidence of your work.
3. **Review.** Share your submission with your mentor and work through their feedback.

Use the “Mark my study complete” button at the end of each lesson as a personal reminder. It saves only in this browser; it is separate from your mentor’s assessment.
